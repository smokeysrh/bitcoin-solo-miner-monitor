# HashHurdr brand policy

Short version: the code is MIT and free for everyone. The brand has a few friendly rules, and the main one is: have fun with Hashling.

This covers the name HashHurdr, the logo and wordmark, the app icon, Hashling the mascot with all his poses, and the other brand art in this kit. Fonts keep their own licenses and the color files follow the app's MIT license.

## The official marks (name, wordmark, logo, app icon)
- Use them as they are. Show them to talk about the project, link to it, review it, or show that your tool works with it.
- Do not change, recolor or redraw them.
- Do not use them as the name or logo of a different product, or of a fork that changed the code.
- Do not suggest that the project endorses you when it doesn't.

## Hashling
- Share him freely.
- Fan art, memes, remixes, videos and clips are welcome for fun and for the community. If it could be mistaken for official art, label it fan-made.
- No scams, rug pulls or fake giveaways. Ever.

## Merch
- Stickers, shirts and similar for fun or at cost are welcome.
- Selling it commercially needs permission first. Ask by opening an issue on the project page.

## Credit and code
- Credit is appreciated and never required.
- The app's code stays MIT. Fork it, change it, ship it. If you ship a fork, give it your own name and logo.

## Rights
The project's owner keeps the rights to the brand art and may update or relax this policy at any time. No trademark registration or legal clearance is claimed. This is a plain-words policy, not legal advice.
